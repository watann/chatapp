ChatApp institution workflows — Implementation and QA — 2026-11-04
===================================================================

DELIVERED CHANGES
- A dedicated study-departments.php page. Students can select only active
  approved study departments when being added to an official registry list.
- Cleaned Central Setup: teacher qualification, teacher specialization,
  staff workplace and organizational setup remain; study departments moved
  out of the mixed option list.
- Default Registry Department in organization hierarchy (code: registry).
- Registration-list workflow with MySQL-backed batches and rows:
  registry drafting -> administration review/edit -> manager final approval.
- Registrar and Administration can add/edit/delete rows at their allowed
  stages. The Administration Head controls its forward/return decision.
- Final manager approval is a single database transaction for the whole
  list. Records are not official until it succeeds.
- History events/notes and distinct pending/returned/approved states.
- Approvals page contains waiting registry lists, in addition to normal
  pre-existing department requests.
- Direct old registration form and endpoint removed/disabled so a person
  cannot bypass the required list workflow.
- Existing official record viewing and account-suspension flow preserved.
- Old pending legacy registration requests are not discarded or deleted.

MIGRATION
Existing database: back up first; run DATABASE_MIGRATION_2026_11_04.sql
in the chatapp database AFTER prior migrations. Do not overwrite existing
DB with chatapp.sql. Fresh database: import chatapp.sql only.

PERMISSIONS
- Registry staff/head: prepare their own lists and submit to Administration.
- Student Affairs: prepare student lists.
- Human Resources: prepare teacher/staff lists.
- Administration staff: edit rows in submitted lists; may prepare own lists.
- Administration Head: edit submitted lists and formally forward/return.
- Institution Manager: view all lists, final approve or return.
- Finance/academic/IT staff: no registration-list access unless separately
  assigned to an authorized department with the required rights.
- An Administration Head may not approve a list that they originally created.
